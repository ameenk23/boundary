# Boundary

**A spatial authoring tool for autonomous mobility research.**

Boundary is a tool for mapping, organising and managing spatial information relevant to autonomous mobility and Operational Design Domains (ODDs).

It brings geographical information and structured data together in one place, making it easier to describe road environments, infrastructure and other conditions that influence where and how autonomous systems can operate.

## Why Boundary?

Working with ODDs and real-world road environments often involves information spread across GIS tools, spreadsheets and technical documentation. Keeping this information consistent and easy to manage can become difficult.

Boundary aims to make this process simpler by combining an interactive map with structured data management.

## What it does

- **Spatial authoring:** Create, view and manage geographical entities on an interactive map.
- **Structured data:** Define and maintain attributes associated with mapped entities.
- **ODD representation:** Organise information relevant to the operational conditions of autonomous systems.
- **Data management:** Edit, update and manage spatial information in one place.
- **Data export:** Export structured information for use in other tools and workflows.

## Current Development

Boundary is actively being developed and is currently used by me and fellow researchers in our autonomous mobility research work.

Development is driven by actual research requirements and feedback from day-to-day use. The tool is continuously evolving as new requirements and use cases emerge.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite
- **Mapping:** Leaflet
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Database:** SQLite, SQLAlchemy

## Project Status

Boundary is an ongoing research and development project. Features and functionality are being added and refined as the platform develops.

## Vision

The goal is to provide a practical way to represent and manage the environments in which autonomous systems operate, connecting geographical information with structured, reusable data.
